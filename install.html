How to Install CORAL on Android

Because CORAL is a Progressive Web App (PWA), you can install it directly on your Android phone through your web browser without paying or downloading anything from the Google Play Store.

Step 1: Open CORAL in Google Chrome

Open your Android phone's web browser and navigate to your deployed CORAL website URL.

Step 2: Tap the Browser Menu

Tap the three vertical dots icon (⋮) in the top-right corner of Chrome.

Step 3: Select "Install App" or "Add to Home Screen"

Tap on "Install app" or "Add to Home screen" from the menu options.

Step 4: Confirm Installation

Confirm by tapping "Install". The CORAL icon will appear on your phone's app drawer!
